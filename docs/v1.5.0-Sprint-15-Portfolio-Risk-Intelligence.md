# v1.5.0, Sprint 15 — Institutional Portfolio & Risk Intelligence Engine

## Non-negotiable, stated up front

**This is not a new portfolio page. This is not a new dashboard.** This
sprint builds the intelligence *layer* that continuously evaluates the
user's entire portfolio, planned trades, and overall risk — sitting above
Research, Notebook, Strategies, Trade Plans, Decision Workflow (Sprint 13),
Execution & Lifecycle Manager (Sprint 14), Trade Journal, Performance,
Portfolio, and Command Centre (Sprint 12). It never re-implements a single
existing portfolio/risk calculation — it composes them.

## What this is

```
Portfolio Health Score  ──┐
Risk Intelligence        ─┼─→  AI Portfolio Coach  ──→  Learning Centre
What-If Analysis         ─┘
```

Concretely, this sprint ships:

1. **`lib/portfolioRiskIntelligence.ts`** — the reusable intelligence
   engine. Given already-fetched data from all 3 engines, it produces:
   - **`computePortfolioHealthScore()`** — a transparent, 12-factor
     cross-engine Health Score (Diversification, Sector Concentration,
     Position Sizing, Cash Allocation, Open Risk, Portfolio Volatility,
     Correlation, Maximum Drawdown, Decision Quality, Trade Quality,
     Journal Completion, Learning Completion). Every factor carries its
     own `score`, `available`, `detail`, and `sourceModule` — **never a
     black-box number.**
   - **`computeRiskIntelligence()`** — 10 institutional risk signals
     (Portfolio Concentration, Sector/Currency/Country Exposure,
     Single-Position Risk, Open Trade Risk, Total Portfolio Risk,
     Correlation Risk, Liquidity Risk, Pending Trade Impact).
   - **`computeWhatIfAnalysis()`** — pure arithmetic evaluating a planned
     (Ready-to-Execute) trade's effect on total open risk, its named
     engine cap, and an approximate worst-case drawdown — **analysis
     only, never execution.**
   - **`buildPortfolioCoachNarrative()`** — a deterministic AI Portfolio
     Coach narrative (health explanation, strongest/weakest
     positions/factors, largest risks, institutional best practice,
     suggested research areas) — mirrors `buildDecisionCoachNarrative()`'s
     (Sprint 13) own established pattern exactly.
2. **`pages/PortfolioRiskIntelligence.tsx`** (`/portfolio-risk-intelligence`)
   — the Health Score card, the Risk Intelligence card, the What-If
   Analysis tool, the AI Portfolio Coach card (with the platform's real
   `AskCoachLauncher` for free-form Q&A), and a Continuous Learning card.
   Every card links out to the real, already-built page for management
   actions (Portfolio Dashboard, Execution & Lifecycle Manager) — it never
   re-implements their own detail views.
3. **Command Centre integration** — a new "Portfolio & Risk Intelligence"
   card surfacing Health Score, Risk Alerts, Exposure, Allocation, Pending
   Trade Impact, Portfolio Trends, and Recommended Review Tasks, reusing
   the exact same `usePortfolioRiskIntelligence()` hook the full page uses
   — never a second, duplicate computation.

## Never fabricated — the honesty audit

A direct repository audit was performed before writing a line of this
sprint's code to confirm exactly what does and does not exist:

- **No statistical correlation coefficient** exists anywhere in this
  codebase for any pair of positions. Correlation is always honestly
  `available: false`; the Correlation Risk *signal* instead surfaces
  Options Income's own real categorical clusters (positions sharing an
  underlying/sector/strategy/expiration) — a genuinely different, weaker
  claim, never presented as a correlation figure.
- **No portfolio-level (weighted, covariance-aware) volatility model**
  exists. Engine 2's Market Regime Engine computes real *per-symbol*
  technical volatility, but blending that into one portfolio figure would
  itself require the correlation data above, which doesn't exist.
  Portfolio Volatility is always honestly `available: false`.
- **No cash-allocation health-scoring formula** exists in any engine.
  Cash Allocation is always honestly `available: false`; a raw,
  *unscored* reference figure (Options Income's buying power as a % of
  portfolio value) is shown only when resolvable, never presented as a
  0-100 health score.
- **No currency or country exposure tracking** exists anywhere — every
  position is implicitly assumed USD-denominated with no domicile
  classification. Both signals are always honestly `available: false`.
- **No portfolio-wide liquidity aggregation** exists — only Engine 2's own
  per-symbol Liquidity Engine (Trading Research → Liquidity tab). Liquidity
  Risk is always honestly `available: false`, with a reason pointing to
  where the per-symbol data does live.
- **Pending Trade Impact is a real count, never a fabricated dollar
  figure** — a Ready-to-Execute trade plan has no numeric risk figure
  until it's actually executed (Sprint 14's own honest design), so this
  signal reports how many plans are pending and points the user to
  What-If Analysis to actually quantify the impact.
- **"Portfolio Trends" is honestly a point-in-time reading.** No
  historical snapshot table exists for this Health Score — the Command
  Centre card says so explicitly rather than fabricating a trend arrow.

With 9 of 12 Health Score factors always potentially available and 3
permanently unavailable by design, `confidenceLevel` self-documents —
the same honesty pattern Investment Quality Engine (Phase 2, Sprint 15)
and Competitive Advantage Engine (Phase 2, Sprint 21) each established for
their own analogous permanently-unavailable metrics.

## Scoring framework

Every blended factor is a plain, unweighted average over whichever inputs
are genuinely available (never averaging in a missing input as 0) —
the same renormalize-over-availability pattern Investment Quality Engine
and Tom Nash Engine (Phase 2) both already established:

- `overall = mean(available factor scores)`, rounded.
- `confidenceLevel`: `High` at ≥80% of factors available, `Moderate` at
  ≥50%, else `Low`.
- `label`: `Excellent` (≥80) / `Strong` (≥65) / `Moderate` (≥50) /
  `Elevated Risk` (≥35) / `Poor` (below).

## Risk model

Every risk signal is either a direct pass-through of an already-computed
engine field (Sector Exposure, Single-Position Risk, Open Trade Risk,
Correlation Risk clusters) or a single, disclosed, plain-arithmetic
combination (Total Portfolio Risk = Options Income + Trading dollars,
**deliberately excluding** the Investing Engine's own typically long-term,
not-risk-budgeted-the-same-way holdings — disclosed directly in the
signal's own detail text, never silently combined).

### What-If Analysis

`computeWhatIfAnalysis()` is pure arithmetic over already-known figures —
no new risk-scoring formula:

- `hypotheticalTotalRiskDollars = currentTotalRiskDollars + hypotheticalRiskDollars`.
- For a Trading-engine trade plan: checked against Trading's own named
  6% portfolio-risk cap (mirrored, not imported, from
  `lib/tradingRisk.ts`'s `MAX_PORTFOLIO_RISK_PCT` — the same
  cross-package "duplicate the constant value, never the logic" precedent
  `lib/investingUniverse.ts`'s `TRADING_MARKET_UNIVERSE` already
  established for a different pair of engines).
- For an Investing-engine trade plan: points to the Investing Engine's own
  40% sector-concentration cap and its own Portfolio Construction risk
  view, since sector-concentration impact for one specific new symbol
  isn't computed here.
- For an Options Income trade plan: honestly states no single named
  portfolio-risk-percentage cap exists for that engine.
- Worst-case drawdown is a **proportional approximation**, scaled from the
  Options Income Engine's own most recent worst stress-test scenario —
  never a fresh re-run of that scenario against the specific hypothetical
  trade, and the result's own `notes[]` says so explicitly.

## Future enhancements

None of this sprint's design blocks a future, explicitly-approved
enhancement — but none is implemented, hinted at, or half-built here
either:

- A real portfolio-level volatility/correlation model would need genuine
  covariance data this platform has never collected — the seam is the
  `portfolio_volatility`/`correlation` factor codes, ready to be filled in
  without touching any other factor's own logic.
- A cash-allocation health-scoring convention (e.g., a target %-of-account
  cash band) could be added as a new named constant exactly like Trading's
  own 6% cap, the moment an owner decision sets one.
- Historical Health Score trend tracking would need a new snapshot table
  (the same shape `intelligence_snapshots`, Phase 16, already established
  for a different engine) — deliberately not built this sprint, since
  "Portfolio Trends" was explicitly honestly disclosed as a gap rather
  than silently promised.

None of the above is scheduled or promised for a specific future sprint —
they are simply the seams this design already leaves open.
