# Learning Centre Sprint L2J — AI Coach & Institutional Mentor Academy

This is an education sprint, not a feature sprint. It teaches users how to work with the platform's existing AI assistants — it does not add, modify, or invent any AI capability. `execution.ts`/`optionsMath.ts`/`risk.ts`/`autoExecution.ts`/`autoAdjustment.ts` and every AI-serving library file (`coachLLM.ts`, `investingCoach.ts`, `tradingCoach.ts`, `institutionalMentor.ts`, `lib/ai-core`, etc.) are completely untouched.

## 1. AI Capability Map

Before writing any content, the platform's entire AI surface was inspected directly via 2 parallel read-only Explore agents (covering complementary halves: Coach/Mentor/Trading/Options modules on one side, narration/summaries/reports/watchlist/dashboard modules on the other), cross-referenced against direct source reading and the existing Learning Centre's own already-written AI-related topics.

### Genuinely LLM-powered (real Anthropic/OpenAI calls via `lib/ai-core`, streaming SSE, honest `source: "llm" | "template"` fallback disclosure)

| Feature | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI Coach | `/assistant` | Mode-selectable chat; only 3 of its ~20 response branches (Explain Trade, Teach Greeks, Value Research) actually call the LLM — most of its "Auto-detect" routing is a keyword dispatcher to deterministic templates. |
| Unified AI Trade Coach | `/ai-trading-coach` (also embedded on `/trading-research`) | Genuinely free-form, persistent server-side chat history, grounded across Engine 2 (market structure/risk/probability) AND Engine 3 (options-income portfolio/scanner/journal) in one composed context. |
| "Ask the AI Investment Analyst" | Value Research page (`/stock-analyst`) | Free-form, grounded in the full report. |
| "Narrate this verdict" | Value Research page (`/stock-analyst`) | Explains only the already-reached Investment Committee consolidated verdict — a narrow narration button, not free-form. |
| AI Market Briefing & Report Comparison narration | Portfolio AI (`/portfolio-ai`) | Narrates already-computed briefing/comparison figures. |
| "Narrate My Day" | Cross-Engine Daily Report (`/daily-report`) | Optional overlay on an always-visible deterministic summary. |
| Management Quality's Communication Quality / Long-Term Focus dimensions | Filings & Mgmt tab, `/stock-analyst` | The platform's single most tightly-guarded AI use case — genuinely LLM-narrated from a company's own SEC filing text, **no fallback score at all** (honest `unavailable` beats a fabricated number), and a structural guard discards any response characterizing a named individual rather than the filing itself. |

### Deterministic / template-based (zero LLM calls, by explicit design)

| Feature | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Institutional AI Coach | `/institutional-ai-coach` | 8 named coaches (Investment/Portfolio/Decision/Valuation/Risk/Research/Monitoring/Committee) over Engine 1's already-computed report figures. |
| Institutional Trading AI Coach | `/trading-ai-coach` | 8 named coaches (Structure/Liquidity/Session/Risk/Trade Plan/Journal/Scenario/Psychology & Discipline) over Engine 2's own workbenches — a **confirmed naming collision, not the same feature**, as the unrelated, genuinely LLM-powered AI Trade Coach above. |
| Institutional Mentor | `/institutional-mentor` | Its own header states plainly: "THIS IS NOT an LLM, a chatbot, predictive AI, financial advice, portfolio optimisation, or a trade recommendation engine." |
| AI Portfolio Analyst | `/portfolio-analyst` | Options-income side, deterministic — a **confirmed naming collision, not the same feature**, as the unrelated, genuinely LLM-narrated Portfolio AI (`/portfolio-ai`). |
| Watchlists Engine's own Coach tab | `/watchlists-engine` | Deterministic. |
| ~12 further topic-keyed "Coach" panels | Rebalancing Engine, Compliance, Scenario Engine, Risk Exposure Engine, Performance Attribution Engine, Decision Support Engine, and similar modules | All deterministic prose over already-computed platform data — none accepts free-form input. |

### Genuine content gaps found (filled this sprint)

- No existing lesson taught users how to **distinguish** genuine LLM output from deterministic templates across this many similarly-named features, or explicitly disclosed the two real naming collisions (Institutional Trading AI Coach vs. AI Trade Coach; AI Portfolio Analyst vs. Portfolio AI).
- No lesson taught **how to write a better prompt** for this platform's own genuinely free-form AI boxes, using real examples of what those boxes can and honestly cannot answer.
- No lesson walked through the **end-to-end AI-assisted research workflow** (Value Research → Statements/Filings/Earnings → coaches → Ask panel → Committee) as one coherent session.
- No lesson walked through the **end-to-end AI-assisted trading workflow** (Trading Research → workbenches → Trading AI Coach → unified AI Trade Coach → Journal).
- No lesson covered **AI-assisted portfolio review** across both accounts (options-income and Investing), including the explicit, disclosed design choice that the two are never blended into one number.
- No lesson taught **how to read an AI response's own honesty signals** — confidence-vs-certainty, SIMULATED/LIVE data-source labels, `source: "llm"|"template"`, and honest `unavailable` results — as a single, transferable skill.
- `trading-ai-coach-overview` (an existing topic in `TRADING_ENGINE_PATH`) existed only as a 905-character plain stub with no module-guide depth, no naming-collision disclosure, and no knowledge check.

No AI capability, agent, dashboard, or API was invented. Every route, coach name, disclaimer constant, and behavior described in the new content was confirmed against real source before being written.

## 2. Lessons created

A new, 14th `LearningPath` — `AI_ACADEMY_PATH` (key `ai-academy`, `glossaryCategory: "platform"`) — since this sprint is explicitly cross-engine, matching `PLATFORM_BASICS_PATH`'s own "how to use the software itself" scope rather than belonging to any single existing engine-specific path.

| Topic key | Title | Maps to requested lesson |
|---|---|---|

All 6 follow the platform's established full module-guide structure: overview, learning objectives (via `whyItMatters`/`whyItExists`/`institutionalThinking`), platform walkthrough (`screenWalkthrough`), AI capability explanation (`body`), step-by-step workflow (`workflowSteps`), 3 `workedExamples` labeled exactly Good/Average/Poor Opportunity (using only real platform examples), `bestPractices` (professional tips), `aiCoachPrompts` (prompt examples), `riskWarnings` (AI safety guidance), `relatedModuleHrefs`/`relatedGlossaryKeys`/`nextStepKeys` (related lessons + glossary links), and a 5-question `knowledgeCheck`. They chain linearly via `nextStepKeys` (Meet Your AI Team → Writing Better Prompts → AI Research Workflow → AI Trading Workflow → AI Portfolio Review → Understanding AI Responses, the terminal lesson).
