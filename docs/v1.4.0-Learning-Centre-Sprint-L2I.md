# Learning Centre Sprint L2I — Institutional Investing Academy

## 1. Platform inspection summary

Before writing any content, the platform's real "Institutional Investing" surface was inspected directly (source reading plus 2 parallel read-only Explore agents covering: (1) Investing Dashboard, Research & Fundamental Analysis, Portfolio Construction, and (2) Portfolio Monitoring/Watchlists/Alerts, Institutional Investment Review/AI Coach/Institutional Mentor/Investment Committee), followed by direct inspection of the existing `INSTITUTIONAL_INVESTING_PATH` (11 pre-existing topics, added Phase 21) to identify what was already covered richly, what existed only as a plain stub, and what had zero coverage at all.

### Key findings

- **Three dashboard-shaped screens exist**, and only one belongs to this sprint's scope: the **Institutional Dashboard** (`/institutional-dashboard`, symbol-driven, cross-engine) is already covered by an existing, rich Platform Basics topic (`institutional-dashboard-overview`) and was deliberately cross-referenced, not duplicated. The **Investing Executive Dashboard** (`/stock-analyst/executive-dashboard`) had zero prior Learning Centre coverage — a genuine gap, filled this sprint. A third, similarly-named **Portfolio Dashboard** (`/portfolio-dashboard`) belongs to the Options Income Engine's own Paper Trading account, confirmed unrelated and flagged for disambiguation rather than taught as the same feature.
- **Research & Fundamental Analysis** (the Value Research page, `/stock-analyst`) has real, genuinely implemented Financial Ratios (Valuation/Profitability/Liquidity & Leverage groupings, with Quick Ratio/ROA/Asset Turnover honestly `available:false`), three independent named valuation models (Graham, DCF, Buffett), a real 5-year Financial Statements tab, a real Peers/Industry Comparison tab, a Filings & Mgmt tab that genuinely fetches a company's real, most recent 10-K from **SEC EDGAR** (independent of the SIMULATED/LIVE fundamentals toggle), and an Earnings Intelligence tab — none of this was previously covered by any Learning Centre topic. This was the single largest content gap found.
- **Portfolio Construction** (`/stock-analyst/portfolio-construction`, on-screen "Institutional Portfolio Manager") is a fully real, wired workflow: create a portfolio, add holdings with target weights, an "Add from Watchlist" equal-weight helper, a genuine drift/rebalance-badge system (`REBALANCE_DRIFT_THRESHOLD_PCT = 5` percentage points), and a Risk tab with two real hard caps (`SINGLE_SYMBOL_CONCENTRATION_CAP_PCT = 25`, `SECTOR_CONCENTRATION_CAP_PCT = 40`) that force-cap the overall risk score at 60 when breached. This page's own header explicitly states it **never places, schedules, or submits any trade**. A separate, philosophy-level topic named "Portfolio Construction" already existed in a different path (`INSTITUTIONAL_PATH`, "Institutional Thinking") — confirmed as a genuinely different scope (general risk-management principle, not this specific page's workflow) and cross-referenced, not duplicated.
- **Portfolio Monitoring / Alerts / Watchlists**: the existing `investing-monitoring` topic (15,356 characters) already comprehensively covers the 14 real alert types, severity levels, the notification bell, and the Monitoring Dashboard — confirmed via direct inspection, not assumed. The **two genuinely distinct watchlist systems** (the flat `value_watchlist` used by Portfolio Construction's "Add from Watchlist," and the separate, named-list `investing_watchlists`/Watchlists Engine) are already honestly documented, together, in an existing topic (`options-scanner-watchlists`) in a different path. The one real, previously-uncovered gap was the standalone **Rebalancing Engine** (`/rebalancing-engine`) — a "planning and analysis only" tool, explicitly zero automatic rebalancing/execution/AI predictions, that reuses Portfolio Construction's own drift math and caps — plus tying that together with Portfolio Construction's own Performance/Snapshots/Timeline review tabs.
- **Institutional Investment Review**: the 8-coach **Institutional AI Coach** (`/institutional-ai-coach`, `investingCoach.ts` — Investment/Portfolio/Decision/Valuation/Risk/Research/Monitoring/Committee) had zero prior Learning Centre coverage — confirmed genuinely distinct from the similarly-structured, differently-named 8-coach Trading Engine AI Coach (`/trading-ai-coach`), which already has its own separate topic. **Institutional Mentor** is already covered richly (`investing-institutional-mentor`, 12,001 characters) and was cross-referenced as the capstone, not duplicated. The **Investment Committee** topic existed only as a 1,029-character plain stub whose `externalHref` pointed to the Investment Committee Workbench page — but the real, genuine LLM-narrated "Narrate this verdict" feature actually lives on the *Value Research page itself* (`/stock-analyst`), not the Workbench. This was a genuine content-accuracy correction, not just an expansion.

### Educational-only / partially-implemented items, honestly disclosed in the new content

- Quick Ratio, Return on Assets, Asset Turnover: honestly `available:false` — the platform lacks full balance-sheet line items to compute them correctly; never approximated.
- Country/currency exposure on Portfolio Construction's Allocation tab: honestly `unavailable`.
- Rebalancing (both on Portfolio Construction and the standalone Rebalancing Engine): planning/labeling only — Buy/Sell/Hold badges and suggested share deltas, never an executed, scheduled, or submitted trade.
- The Rebalancing Engine's own scope boundary: only applies to Investing-side (target-weight) portfolios — Trading Engine and Options Income Engine positions have no stored target-weight field anywhere in the codebase.
- "AI insights" across this scope are, without exception, deterministic and template-based — including the Rebalancing Engine's own Coach panel, explicitly labeled "the deterministic AI Coach" in its own code — except the one genuine, working LLM feature: the Investment Committee's "Narrate this verdict" button.

No dashboards, metrics, screens, AI capabilities, broker integrations, or workflows were invented. Every route, constant, and feature name in the new/upgraded content was confirmed against real source before being written.
