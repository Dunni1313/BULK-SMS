# v1.6.0 — Beta Feedback Questions

**Type:** Documentation and product-planning exercise only — no code
changes, per the same disclosure in `docs/v1.6.0-Beta-Testing-Guide.md`.

**How to use this:** hand these to a tester *after* they've completed the
free-form journeys and self-report checklist in
`docs/v1.6.0-Beta-Testing-Guide.md` — these questions are meant to
surface reflection, not first impressions (the earlier documents already
capture those). Deliberately excludes any question about missing
features or "what would you add" — a beta at this stage should surface
whether what already exists is usable, not become a feature-request
channel.

---

## Navigation

1. When you wanted to go somewhere specific in the platform, did you
   reach for the sidebar, the search (⌘K/Ctrl+K), or a link on the page
   itself most often — and why?
2. Was there ever a moment where two sidebar items sounded like they
   might do the same thing? If so, which ones?
3. Did the sidebar ever feel like it had "too much" in it, or did the
   grouping make sense to you?

## Clarity

4. Think of one page where you understood immediately what it was for.
   What made that page clear?
5. Think of one page where you weren't sure what it was for at first.
   What would have made it clearer in that first moment?
6. Did any badge, label, or status (e.g. "Blocked," "SIMULATED,"
   "Advisory Only") make you stop and wonder what it actually meant?

## Workflow

7. Walking from research to a decision to (simulated) execution — did
   that feel like one connected process, or like separate tools you had
   to stitch together yourself?
8. Was there a point where you expected a button or link to take you
   somewhere specific, and it took you somewhere else (or nowhere)?
9. If you paused your session and came back later, would you know where
   you left off?

## Learning

10. Did you use the "Learn" button/link anywhere? If so, did it actually
    answer your question?
11. Did you feel like you needed outside knowledge (e.g., knowing what
    "delta" or "DTE" means) that the platform didn't explain when you
    needed it?
12. Did the AI Trading Coach ever teach you something about your own
    process, or did it only tell you what to click next?

## Confidence

13. Was there a moment where you weren't sure if an action you took was
    real or simulated?
14. Did any number on the screen (a score, a risk level, a P&L) make you
    second-guess whether it was accurate, and why?
15. After using the platform for this session, would you trust it enough
    to use daily for real research — even in Paper Trading mode? Why or
    why not?

## Professional Feel

16. Did the platform feel consistent from page to page, or did some
    pages feel more "finished" than others? Name one of each if you can.
17. If you showed this to a colleague who trades or invests
    professionally, what's the first thing you think they'd comment on —
    positively or negatively?

## Ease of Use

18. What's the single most annoying or frustrating moment from your
    session today?
19. What's the single easiest, smoothest moment from your session today?
20. If you had to describe today's experience in one sentence to someone
    who's never seen this platform, what would you say?
