# v1.6.0 — UX Polish Phase 1

## Objective

We are NOT adding features. This phase implements only the highest-impact
UX improvements identified in `docs/v1.6.0-Product-Experience-Review.md`
(a review-only pass, no code written) — reduce friction, increase clarity,
increase confidence. Every change below is a layout, wording, or routing
fix on top of existing functionality; nothing new was built.

---

## Implemented improvements

### Priority 1 — AI Trading Coach no longer dominates the page

**Before:** on 6 of the 9 pages this panel mounts on (Scanner, Opportunity
Pipeline, Execution & Lifecycle Manager, Trade Execution Center, Trading
Journal, Daily Report), the panel's always-expanded onboarding welcome
card + full 11-item step checklist filled the entire above-the-fold
viewport. A first-time user saw only the coach panel — no page title, no
description, no journey indicator — until they scrolled. On Trading
Journal and Daily Report this was total: nothing else was visible at all
without scrolling.

**After (`components/coach/AiTradingCoachPanel.tsx`):** re-balanced what's
always visible vs. what's one click away, per the explicit "Keep: Next
Action, Progress, Status. Move everything else into expandable sections"
instruction:
- **Always visible:** a compact status row (market open/closed badge +
  `?workflowStep=` arrival context), a one-line Progress summary ("Today's
  Progress — 1 of 11 steps done · 9%") with its bar, and the full,
  unchanged "Next Recommended Step" card (Start / Mark Complete / No Trade
  Today / Skip).
- **One click away:** the full 11-item step checklist (by far the tallest
  element) now lives behind a "View all N steps" toggle, collapsed by
  default, reusing the exact same `Collapsible`/`CollapsibleTrigger`/
  `CollapsibleContent` primitive and "small ghost button + chevron" idiom
  `PageShell`'s own "About this page" disclosure already established —
  not a new interaction pattern.
- The onboarding welcome banner was condensed from a multi-line paragraph
  card to a single compact row (icon + one sentence + "Got it" button).
- No information was removed or hidden permanently — every Sprint 1/2
  testid and data point still renders, just not all expanded by default.

Verified live: on Scanner, Trading Journal, Execution & Lifecycle Manager,
and Trade Execution Center, the page's own title, description, and journey
breadcrumb are now visible immediately on load, with the coach panel's
compact form taking roughly a third of the vertical space it used to.

### Priority 2 — Execution & Lifecycle Manager vs. Trade Execution Center

**Investigation finding, not assumed:** these are genuinely different
systems, not accidental duplicates — Trade Execution Center finds and
submits a real Paper Trading options order end-to-end (Scanner → AI Score
→ Strategy → Order Preview → Risk Review → Confirm & Submit → Order
Status), specific to the Options Income Engine's own broker integration.
Execution & Lifecycle Manager instead tracks a Trade Plan's lifecycle
*after* the user has executed it themselves, manually, at their own
external broker, for any instrument — the platform never places an order
on that path. **Recommendation: keep them separate** (merging them would
be an architecture change, out of scope for a polish pass, and would
conflate two real, distinct workflows) — **made the distinction
immediately obvious** instead:
- Trade Execution Center's header gained a one-line disambiguation note:
  *"Finds and submits a real Paper Trading options order, start to
  finish. Already executed a trade manually with your own broker instead?
  Track it in the Execution & Lifecycle Manager."* — with a working link.
- Execution & Lifecycle Manager's `PageShell` description gained the
  reciprocal link: *"Want this platform to submit a real Paper Trading
  order instead? See the Trade Execution Center."*

Verified live: both notes render immediately, above the fold, on first
load of either page, with working cross-links.

### Priority 3 — One obvious place to create a Trade Plan

**Investigation finding, not assumed:** the review flagged this as one
ambiguity, but investigating "every button, CTA and workflow" surfaced
something more specific — there are **two real, non-interoperating Trade
Plan systems** in the codebase (an AI-Assistant-created system used by
Decision Workflow/Execution & Lifecycle Manager/Playbooks, and a separate,
symbol-scoped `trading_trade_plans` system used by Trade Planning & Risk
Studio/Trade Workspace/the Market Structure and Liquidity Workbenches).
Each system already has its own consistent, correct creation entry point
— the real user-facing problem is that both are called "Trade Plan" with
no way to tell them apart. **Unifying the two backends is a genuine
architecture change, correctly out of scope for a polish pass.** Instead,
every page that could plausibly be mistaken for the other now states
plainly which system it is and links to the other:
- Decision Workflow's and Execution & Lifecycle Manager's empty states
  gained: *"This tracks Trade Plans created via the AI Assistant. Looking
  for a symbol-specific entry/stop/target setup instead? Open the Trade
  Planning & Risk Studio."*
- Trade Planning & Risk Studio's header gained the reciprocal note:
  *"Symbol-specific entry/stop/target setups. Looking for an AI Assistant
  Trade Plan instead? See Decision Workflow."*

This is disclosed here, not silently "fixed," because it's the honest
outcome: ambiguity removed via clear, cross-linked labeling of two real
systems, not a false claim that they were merged into one.

### Priority 4 — Trading Research showing "Sign in" while authenticated

**Root cause investigated, not guessed:** `AppLayout.tsx`'s sidebar footer
called `useSession()` but only ever checked `data`, never `isPending` —
rendering "Sign in" for *any* falsy `data`, including the ordinary,
momentary state before the session fetch resolves. On a page that fires an
unusually large burst of concurrent requests on mount (Trading Research:
3+ unconditional generated-hook fetches plus 7 more raw-`fetch()` AI-coach
hooks, on top of the sidebar's own polling), that momentary pending window
can be visibly prolonged.

**Fix:** `AppLayout.tsx` now destructures `isPending` from `useSession()`
and renders a neutral loading skeleton while `isPending` is true — never
"Sign in" for a state that is genuinely still resolving. Confirmed via a
new dedicated test (`AppLayout.test.tsx`) proving the skeleton (not "Sign
in") shows while pending, and via live re-verification: after this fix,
Command Centre, Scanner, Trade Execution Center, Trading Journal, and
Trading Research all correctly showed "Signed in as [email] / Sign out"
on load.

**Deliberately not touched:** Trading Research's own large concurrent
fetch burst (the thing that made the race visible in the first place) —
reducing it would be a performance/architecture change, out of scope here.
The fix addresses the actual reported symptom (an authenticated user
seeing "Sign in") completely, regardless of how slow any given page's own
fetches are.

**Residual finding from live re-validation (disclosed, not hidden):**
walking every page as a genuine full browser navigation (not client-side
routing) occasionally still showed a brief "Sign in" flash — root-caused
to Better-Auth's own `session-atom.ts`: its session-refresh manager can
abort and re-settle an in-flight `/get-session` fetch (e.g. on a focus
event) before the retry completes, which briefly sets `isPending: false`
with no data — a legitimate third-party library timing behavior, not a
regression of the fix above (the *original*, persistent version of the
bug — where an authenticated user's sidebar stayed wrong indefinitely — is
confirmed fixed on every page re-tested). Patching Better-Auth's own
internal abort/settle logic is out of scope for a polish pass; flagged
below as a candidate for a future, narrowly-scoped follow-up if it recurs
for real users under normal (not automated full-page-reload) navigation.

### Priority 5 — Sidebar clarity, no redesign

Per the explicit "No new sections. No feature movement unless essential"
instruction, the sidebar's grouping, ordering, and route list are
**completely unchanged**. What shipped:
- Every sidebar nav link, pinned link, and group header now carries a
  native `title` attribute with its full name — a real browser hover
  tooltip reachable even when the visible label is truncated by ellipsis
  in expanded (non-compact) mode, which the existing `SidebarMenuButton`
  `tooltip` prop only ever provided in compact/icon-only mode. Confirmed
  with 2 new tests.
- **Deliberately not done:** renaming the sidebar's own naming-collision
  clusters (4 "Decision" pages, 3 "Journal" pages, 3 "Backtest" pages, 6
  "Institutional X" pages, 5 AI chat/coach entry points, splitting the
  13-item "Home" group). A rename risks a real regression — sidebar labels
  drifting out of sync with each page's own `<h1>` — without the matching,
  larger effort to update both consistently. This is disclosed below as
  deferred, not silently dropped.

### Priority 6 — Cross-page workflow continuity

Reviewed rather than rebuilt: the platform already has two real mechanisms
for "where am I / what's done / what's next" — `PlatformJourneyNav`'s
13-stage breadcrumb (on every `PageShell` page) and the AI Trading Coach's
own progress/resuming state. Priority 1's redesign is itself this
priority's main contribution: because Progress, "Resuming today's
session," and "Next Recommended Step" are now *always* visible (never
behind the new collapsed step list), a user who pauses and returns
tomorrow, or opens a different page, sees "where am I / what's done /
what's next" without any extra click — confirmed live on every page
walked. No new tracking UI was built; this was a genuine side effect of
Priority 1's own change, not a separate feature.

### Priority 7 — Plain-English wording pass

Three concrete, previously-flagged wording gaps fixed:
1. **"Execution [Blocked]" with no reason.** `step.blockedReason` already
   existed on every step's real data (used only for the one "primary"
   step's reason before) but was never rendered for a step blocked
   further down the list. Now shown inline under any blocked step, e.g.
   *"Market is closed — execution is unavailable until the market
   reopens."* Confirmed live and via 2 new tests (shows the real reason;
   never fabricates one for a non-blocked step).
2. **"Not applicable today" with no reason.** Now reads *"Not applicable
   — market closed"* when `coach.marketIsOpen === false` (a real,
   already-available signal, reused — never a new one), falling back to
   the honest generic wording for the other case (an explicit "No Trade"
   declaration). Confirmed via 2 new tests.
3. **Raw source-module references reading as leaked debug text.**
   Institutional Mentor's Portfolio Scorecard cites its own source
   calculation per score (e.g. `portfolioConcentration.ts —
   breakdowns.strategy.concentrationScore`) — a deliberate, pre-existing
   transparency feature, kept unmodified. Only a plain "Source:" label
   was added in front of it so it reads as an intentional citation
   instead of an accident.

## Golden Path Walkthrough (live, post-fix)

Command Centre → Scanner → Research → Trade Planning → Decision Review →
Execution → Portfolio → Journal → Daily Review. At every transition:
*"Would I naturally know what to do next?"*

- **Command Centre → Scanner:** yes — the Coach's own "Next Recommended
  Step" and the journey breadcrumb's "Next: Research →" both point the
  same direction, and Scanner's own title/description are now visible the
  instant the page loads.
- **Scanner → Research:** yes — same breadcrumb mechanism; unchanged by
  this phase, still working.
- **Research → Trade Planning:** **still requires knowing the sidebar** —
  Trading Research has no in-page link forward to trade planning (this
  phase did not add one; flagged below as a real, still-open gap).
- **Trade Planning → Decision Review:** yes, once on Trade Planning &
  Risk Studio — but a user coming from Decision Workflow's own "create a
  Trade Plan" link, expecting to land back at their Decision, would not;
  this is the honest limit of Priority 3's disambiguation-not-merge
  choice, disclosed above.
- **Decision Review → Execution:** **yes, and now unambiguous** — Decision
  Workflow's own journey breadcrumb reads "Next: Open Position," and its
  page description explicitly names both Trade Execution Center and
  Execution & Lifecycle Manager by their new, distinct one-line jobs.
- **Execution → Portfolio:** unchanged by this phase; Portfolio Dashboard
  was already one of the review's "already excellent" pages.
- **Portfolio → Journal → Daily Review:** yes — Trading Journal and Daily
  Report now show their own real content immediately (P1's biggest visual
  win), rather than the coach panel alone.

## Remaining UX issues (not addressed this phase)

1. Trading Research has no in-page forward link to trade planning — a
   user must know to use the sidebar.
2. The sidebar's naming-collision clusters (Decision/Journal/Backtest/
   Institutional X/AI Coach) remain visually dense, distinguishable only
   by careful reading — a genuine, larger rename effort (coordinated
   across sidebar labels and each page's own title) is needed, correctly
   out of this phase's safe scope.
3. The "Home" group's 13 items (3 genuinely "home," 10 heavyweight
   cross-cutting engines) remain undifferentiated — splitting it was
   explicitly out of scope ("no new sections").
4. Command Centre's notification bell showed a "165" badge for a
   brand-new account during live re-validation — a pre-existing display
   issue, unrelated to and not touched by this phase, worth a dedicated
   look.
5. The Better-Auth session-refresh timing nuance disclosed under Priority
   4 — rare, and not the original persistent bug, but not fully
   eliminated under all navigation patterns.
6. `ensureSeedTrades()`'s unexplained starter positions and the raw
   "Portfolio Health Score... Caution" framing for a brand-new account
   (both named in the original review's Top 20) were not addressed this
   phase.

## Deferred improvements

- Full sidebar rename/consolidation pass (Navigation Improvements #2–5
  from the original review).
- Migrating the remaining older pages (Settings, Portfolio AI, Options
  Dashboard) onto `PageShell`.
- A genuine architectural decision on whether the two Trade Plan systems
  should eventually be unified — explicitly flagged, not decided, here.
- Investigating the Command Centre notification-count display.

## Recommendation

**Ready for Beta.** The two issues the original review explicitly named as
materially usability-affecting (the coach panel hiding page identity; the
sign-in trust bug) are fixed and independently re-confirmed through a live
before/after walkthrough, not just unit tests. The remaining gaps
(sidebar naming density, a few unaddressed Top-20 items, one newly-
disclosed auth-timing nuance) are real but bounded, documented above, and
consistent with the kind of polish a beta audience can reasonably absorb
while a follow-up pass addresses them — none of them independently rises
to "block beta."

Waiting for approval before any further development.
