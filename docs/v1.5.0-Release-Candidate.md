## 1. Platform overview

DK AI is a three-engine institutional investing/trading platform:

- **Engine 3 — Options Income Engine.** The original, mature system this
  platform was built on: scanner, strategy builder, Greeks, income
  optimisation, risk analytics, automation (with an explicit kill switch),
  and an AI options coach.
- **Engine 1 — Institutional Investing Engine.** Fundamental research,
  valuation (Graham/DCF/Buffett/Tom Nash + a consolidated Investment
  Committee), portfolio construction, decision workflow, and an AI research
  coach.
- **Engine 2 — Institutional Trading Engine.** Market structure, liquidity,
  multi-timeframe trend, regime detection, probability, risk management, a
  trading journal, and an AI trade coach.

A fourth, platform-wide layer — added across v1.5.0's own 21 sprints —
connects all three engines without duplicating any of them: a Command
Centre, Decision Workflow, Execution & Lifecycle Manager, Portfolio & Risk
Intelligence, Workflow Automation, a Knowledge Graph, Playbooks, Decision
Quality Review, Market Intelligence, and the Opportunity Pipeline (Sprint
21). Every one of these composes already-computed data from the three
engines; none re-implements an engine's own calculations.

**Discipline that has held from Sprint 1 through Sprint 21:** the protected
files (`execution.ts`, `optionsMath.ts`, `risk.ts`, `autoExecution.ts`,
`autoAdjustment.ts`) have a confirmed zero-line diff across this entire
range. Every new module states, in its own header comment, what it reuses
and what (if anything) is genuinely new — a pattern this audit found to be
unusually consistently followed (see §7, Code quality observations).

## 2. Architecture summary

- **Monorepo, pnpm workspaces.** Backend `artifacts/api-server` (Express),
  frontend `artifacts/ravish-trading` (React 19 + Vite), E2E
  `artifacts/e2e` (Playwright), shared `lib/db` (Drizzle/Postgres),
  `lib/api-spec` + `lib/api-zod` + `lib/api-client-react` (OpenAPI-driven
  codegen).
- **Auth:** Better-Auth, real sessions, `getScopedUserId()`/
  `getScopedUserId(req)` + `and(eq(id), eq(userId))` ownership-scoping on
  every user-scoped route — a spot-check across the backend audit found no
  unscoped route on a user-owned table.
- **AI narration:** a shared `lib/ai-core` + `coachLLM.ts` pattern
  (`narrate()`/`narrateStream()`) guarantees a disclaimer on every LLM
  narration, with a deterministic template fallback whenever no LLM key is
  configured — reused by every coach in the platform (options, investing,
  trading, plus the newer domain-specific narrations), never re-implemented
  per module.
- **Database:** 45 hand-reviewed manual migrations (`000`–`044`,
  nullable→backfill→enforce discipline for anything touching existing
  data), 70 exported tables. The audit found no genuinely orphaned table —
  the codebase's one-table-per-dedicated-module pattern is real, not
  accidental sprawl, and every user-scoped table already has an index on
  its `userId`/parent-FK column.
- **Frontend routing:** 101 routes (97 distinct page components, 4 reused
  across more than one path — e.g. Learning Paths, Trade Ticket — a
  deliberate "move, cross-link, never delete" pattern rather than dead
  code), 100% `React.lazy()`-loaded since the Sprint 53 code-splitting
  pass.

## 4. User workflows

The platform's own intended entry sequence, per its Command Centre header
copy and the sprints that built it:

1. **Start at the Command Centre (`/`).** Daily Briefing → today's
   Workflow → Quick Actions.
2. **Discover.** Opportunity Pipeline surfaces candidates from Market
   Intelligence, watchlist target crossings, stale research, portfolio
   gaps, and Knowledge Graph themes — never a trading signal.
3. **Research.** Promote a candidate into the Research Terminal /
   Research Workspace; the AI Coach and Learning Centre are available
   throughout, contextually.
4. **Decide.** Decision Workflow / Decision Engine / Investment Committee
   produce a deterministic verdict with full evidence attribution.
5. **Plan and (manually) execute.** Trade Planning & Risk Studio / Trade
   Execution Center / Order Preview — every execution path stays
   Paper-Trading-by-default; live trading requires an explicit setting.
6. **Track.** Execution & Lifecycle Manager, the Trading/Options Journals,
   Portfolio & Risk Intelligence.
7. **Review.** Decision Quality & Review evaluates process, not P&L
   outcome; Playbooks and the Learning Centre close the loop for next
   time.

## 5. Consolidation applied this sprint (Phase 2)

All changes below are additive polish or behavior-preserving refactors —
none change what any module computes.

- **3 N+1 query fixes** (measurable, not speculative): `routes/aiNotebooks.ts`'s
  `buildNotebookContext()` batched a per-conversation-id loop into one
  `inArray()` lookup; `routes/tradePlans.ts` and `routes/aiStrategies.ts`'s
  version-restore paths batched per-row inserts into one `.values([...])`
  call each.
- **Design-consistency fixes:** `TradingResearch.tsx`'s and
  `PortfolioAI.tsx`'s page headers standardized onto the platform's own
  `text-2xl font-bold text-foreground` h1 convention (the latter also
  dropped a hardcoded `text-white`, which would have been unreadable in
  light mode).
- **Accessibility fixes:** added `aria-label` to 6 genuinely unlabeled
  icon-only buttons (`PortfolioWorkspace.tsx` ×2, `Scanner.tsx` ×2,
  `TradeWorkspace.tsx` ×2). A broader audit sample of 26 candidate buttons
  found 20 already correctly labeled — the earlier heuristic (a fixed
  character-window grep) produced false positives on multi-line JSX
  attribute blocks; the true gap was smaller than first estimated.
- **Command Centre reorganization:** its ~16 panels were regrouped under 4
  labeled sections ("Market & Portfolio Snapshot," "Decisions & Execution
  In Progress," "Research, Intelligence & Opportunities," "Portfolio
  Intelligence, Learning & Coaching") instead of one long, sprint-chronological
  stack — no panel removed, reordered internally, or changed in what it
  computes; confirmed behavior-preserving against the existing
  `InstitutionalCommandCentre.test.tsx` suite (35 tests, unmodified,
  passing).
- **One duplication deliberately left unmerged, disclosed rather than
  silently fixed:** `components/coach/TradingCoachEmptyState.tsx` visually
  resembles `lib/ai-coach/WorkspaceEmptyState.tsx`, but the two need
  different layouts (a full-height centered chat placeholder vs. a compact
  dashed-border list placeholder) — merging them would require adding
  several new optional props for a single caller, which is premature
  abstraction, not simplification. Left as two small, honest components.
- **Two "text-xl vs text-2xl" items flagged by the initial audit sweep were
  investigated and found NOT to be drift** — `OptionsLifecycleManager.tsx`
  and `LiquidityWorkbench.tsx` correctly follow the smaller `text-xl`
  convention shared by every other "Workbench/Studio" page family member
  (confirmed against `MarketStructureWorkbench.tsx`, `TradePlanningStudio.tsx`,
  `StrategyWorkbench.tsx`) — left unchanged.

## 6. Known limitations (honestly disclosed, not fixed this sprint)

- **Error-state coverage gap.** Only ~44 of 86 sampled non-test pages check
  `isError` from their own data-fetching hooks. A failed fetch on the
  remaining pages likely renders as a perpetually-loading skeleton or a
  silently blank section rather than a visible error message. This is a
  real, sizeable UX gap — fixing it properly means touching dozens of
  pages individually, which is out of this consolidation sprint's own
  "polish, don't rebuild" scope; flagged here as the top UX-quality item
  for a future dedicated pass. **[Sprint 23]** The 3 highest-value pages in
  the platform's own core workflow chain — `MarketIntelligence.tsx`,
  `OpportunityPipeline.tsx`, `PortfolioRiskIntelligence.tsx` — now have a
  real error banner. The remainder is still open; see
  `docs/v1.5.0-General-Availability.md` §2 for the full, re-verified
  remaining scope.
- **`PaperPortfolio.tsx` has no page-level loading gate** — its three
  `Loader2`/`animate-spin` usages are all scoped to individual "Refresh"
  buttons, not a top-level skeleton while the page's own initial data is
  still resolving. A smaller, related instance of the error-state gap
  above.
- **Test coverage gaps, backend:** several large, foundational `lib/*.ts`
  modules have no dedicated unit-test file of their own (`fundamentals.ts`,
  `coachLLM.ts`, `execution.ts`, `coach.ts`, `decisionSupportEngine.ts`,
  `performanceAttribution.ts`, `optionsMath.ts`, `autoExecution.ts`,
  `valueInvesting.ts`, and ~25 others) — most are exercised indirectly via
  integration/route tests, but a dedicated unit-test pass would close a
  real gap, especially for the protected files.
- **Test coverage gaps, routes:** 19 route files have no dedicated
  `*.route.test.ts`, including some of the largest and oldest
  (`stockAnalyst.ts` at 1381 lines, `ai.ts`, `coach.ts`, `trades.ts`,
  `portfolioAI.ts`) — likely covered by the broader `tenantIsolation.test.ts`/
  `auth.test.ts` IDOR sweeps, but not by a dedicated functional test suite.
- **E2E coverage is a thin smoke layer.** 5 Playwright specs cover
  signup/login plus one representative flow per engine, plus 2 cross-engine
  composition views. Zero E2E coverage exists for: order execution/trade
  lifecycle, the auto-execution/adjustment scheduler, options
  backtest/lifecycle management, decision workflow, opportunity pipeline,
  compliance engine, journal/notes, watchlists, notifications, settings/
  broker-connection flows, rebalancing, portfolio construction/optimisation,
  and the scenario/stress-test engines.
- **`REQUIRE_AUTH` defaults to `false`.** When unset, every unauthenticated
  request is served against a shared "legacy owner" identity rather than
  rejected. This is a documented, intentional rollback switch from Phase 1
  — but it must be explicitly verified as `true` before any real
  multi-tenant production deployment; shipping v1.5.0 with it unset would
  silently share one account across every visitor.
- **`learningPaths.ts` is ~9,000 lines**, roughly 6× the next-largest
  backend file — almost entirely lesson/quiz content, not logic, but a
  genuine maintainability concern for anyone editing it going forward.
- **Two full CRUD stacks exist for conceptually similar entities**
  (`routes/journal.ts` vs `routes/tradingJournal.ts`; `routes/tradePlans.ts`
  vs `routes/tradingTradePlans.ts`) — each disclosed in its own header
  comment as deliberately mirroring the other's pattern for a genuinely
  distinct engine/table, not an accidental duplicate, but worth a future
  sprint's evaluation of whether a shared generic CRUD factory would now
  pay for itself. `routes/tradeJournal.ts` (a third, thinner, read-only
  journal surface) looks like the best candidate for retirement.
- **`routes/backtest.ts`** (the original options-side, fabricated-statistics
  equity-curve generator) remains a parallel, intentionally-untouched
  legacy path alongside the newer, genuinely-simulated `optionsBacktest.ts`
  — a disclosed, not-yet-consolidated duplicate.
- **One pre-existing, date-drift test bug found during this sprint's own
  validation runs, disclosed rather than fixed:**
  `lib/portfolioEventRisk.test.ts`'s "reports Consider Review guidance for a
  medium-risk, macro-only position" case reproduces consistently against
  the current date (expects `"medium"`, receives `"high"`) — the fixture's
  hardcoded event date has drifted into a different days-until-event
  bucket since the test was written. Confirmed via `git status --porcelain`
  that neither `portfolioEventRisk.ts` nor its test file were touched this
  sprint; not fixed here since it means editing live risk-bucketing logic
  outside this sprint's "safe, targeted changes only" scope — flagged for
  the next sprint that touches that module, following the same
  date-drift-fixture pattern already resolved once before in
  `docs/V1-Test-Resolution-Report.md`. **[Sprint 23] Fixed** — a second,
  independently-verified frozen clock was added scoped only to that one
  describe block; see `docs/v1.5.0-General-Availability.md` §1.
- **A second, similarly pre-existing and deterministic (not flaky —
  reproduces identically in isolation) failure was found during the same
  validation runs:** `lib/portfolioDashboard.test.ts`'s "largest position
  and largest risk contributor both identify the single trade" case
  expects `deltaSharePct` to be `100`, receives `0`. Confirmed via
  `git status --porcelain` that neither `portfolioDashboard.ts` nor its
  test file were touched this sprint or by any of this sprint's changes —
  not fixed here for the same reason as the `portfolioEventRisk.test.ts`
  item above (live business logic outside this sprint's safe/targeted
  scope). Both items are flagged together as the sprint's own "testing
  gaps" finding, worth investigating together since both surfaced in the
  same validation pass and both involve a single-position/single-trade
  fixture. **[Sprint 23] Fixed** — a genuine production bug: a lone
  position's delta-share was falling back to `0` instead of the honest
  `100` when its own net delta happened to be exactly zero (a
  delta-neutral structure); see `docs/v1.5.0-General-Availability.md` §1.
- **A hand-copied hook pattern** (`useDecisionWorkflow.ts`/
  `useTradeLifecycle.ts`/`usePlaybooks.ts`/`useWorkflowAutomation.ts`/
  `useDecisionQualityEngine.ts`/`useKnowledgeGraph.ts`/
  `useMarketIntelligence.ts`/`useOpportunityPipeline.ts`/
  `usePortfolioRiskIntelligence.ts`) shares an almost-identical
  `loading`/`reloadTick`/state shape across ~9 files, each with a comment
  citing the one it mirrors — a real candidate for a shared generic hook
  factory in a future sprint, not attempted here to avoid the risk of
  touching 9 already-working, already-tested hooks in one pass.

## 9. Future roadmap (Version 2.0 candidates)

Not committed, not scoped — candidates surfaced by this audit for a future,
separately-approved planning pass:

1. **Error/empty-state UX sweep** across the ~42 pages missing visible
   fetch-failure handling — the single highest-leverage UX improvement
   identified.
2. **E2E expansion** to cover trade execution, the automation scheduler,
   and the remaining ~15 major workflows with zero coverage today.
3. **Dedicated unit-test pass** for the largest untested `lib/` modules,
   prioritizing the ones nearest the protected files.
4. **A shared generic data-composition hook factory** to replace the
   9-file hand-copied `loading`/`reloadTick` pattern.
5. **CRUD-stack consolidation evaluation** for the journal/trade-plan
   duplicate pairs, and retirement of the thinner `routes/tradeJournal.ts`
   read-only surface.
6. **`learningPaths.ts` file-splitting** for maintainability (content
   only, no behavior change).
7. **A genuine AI Chief Investment Officer orchestration layer** (the
   originally-proposed Sprint 22 scope, deferred in favor of this
   consolidation pass) — coordinating existing modules into one
   evidence-first investment-committee-style conversation, never
   replacing any specialist coach.
