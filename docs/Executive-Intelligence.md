# Institutional Executive Intelligence & Reporting Hub

Phase 33. A deterministic, cross-engine executive view that unifies the
Institutional Investing Engine and the Institutional Trading Engine into
one screen — an Executive Overview, per-engine summaries, cross-engine
Strategy/Portfolio/Risk/Learning/AI-Coach rollups, a Reporting Centre
tally, and a chronological Activity Timeline.

**This is an aggregation, reporting, and executive-workflow phase, not a
strategy or signal phase.** Nothing here implements or evaluates ICT, SMC,
ASAD, Trader Bill's method, Tom Nash's method, the Dunni Framework, or any
other named methodology. No automated entries or exits, no trading
signals, no forecasting, no AI predictions, no probability engines, no
broker execution. Every figure on this hub is a direct read, count, tally,
or simple aggregate of data already generated by using the platform —
never a new calculation, score, or forecast.

## Where to find it

`/executive-intelligence`, linked from the sidebar navigation, the Command
Palette (inherits the nav entry automatically), the Investing Executive
Dashboard's own Cross-Engine Snapshot section, the Institutional Dashboard
(Trading Engine), the Learning Centre overview, and the Reporting Centre's
own report-type selector.

## What it is

Five tabs:

- **Overview** — top-level KPIs (portfolios, trades reviewed, committee
  snapshots, strategies registered, reports generated) plus five folded
  summary cards: Strategy Summary (a pass-through of the Trading Engine's
  own already-computed strategy/checklist usage), Portfolio Summary (a
  pass-through of the Investing Engine's own already-computed portfolio
  analytics), AI Coach Summary (a correctly-deduplicated cross-engine
  coach-view total — see below), Investing Summary, and Trading Summary.
- **Activity** — a chronological merge of committee snapshots, risk
  snapshots, optimisation reviews, research notes, journal entries, saved
  reports, and notifications, newest first, each with a deep link back to
  its own detail page.
- **Reporting** — a tally of every report you've generated, by type, plus
  your most recently saved reports, with links into the Reporting Centre
  and a shortcut to generate the new Executive Intelligence Summary Report.
- **Learning** — lessons/glossary/strategies/coaches viewed, completed vs.
  remaining learning topics, and your weakest topics (below 50% complete,
  sorted lowest-first) — never a prediction of what to study next, just an
  honest sort of your own already-computed progress across **both**
  engines' shared learning catalog.
- **Risk** — a small cross-engine rollup: Investing's own saved Portfolio
  Risk snapshots (count + most recent score) alongside Trading's own real,
  already-recorded open-position stop/target discipline. Never a re-run of
  either engine's own live risk analysis.

## How the AI Coach total avoids double-counting

Both the Investing Engine's 8 coaches (`investingCoach.ts`) and the Trading
Engine's 9 coaches (`tradingCoach.ts`) record their "mark as viewed" events
to the same shared `learning_progress` table, using the same `itemType:
"coach"` value — and both engines happen to have their own "Risk" coach.
`lib/investingAnalytics.ts`'s own `buildCoachAnalytics()` and
`lib/tradingAnalytics.ts`'s own `buildCoachAnalytics()` (Phase 32,
unmodified) each independently filter every row to only their own engine's
coach-type vocabulary before counting, so a Trading Engine coach view is
never counted as an Investing Engine one or vice versa. The Executive
layer additionally derives "Trading coach views" as the sum of Trading's
own `byType` breakdown (which is correctly per-coach-filtered) rather than
its `totalCoachViews` field (a pre-existing, unmodified Phase 32 behavior
that counts every `itemType: "coach"` row regardless of engine) — avoiding
any double-counting without touching Phase 32's already-shipped code.

## Reporting Centre integration

A new **Executive Intelligence Summary Report** in the Reporting Centre
(`/reporting-centre?reportType=executive-intelligence-summary`), reusing
the existing report framework — the same `InstitutionalReport`/section
shape every other report already uses, generated on demand, savable, and
comparable in the same "generate → review → save" workflow.

## What it deliberately does not do

- No trading signal, probability, or prediction of any kind, anywhere on
  this hub.
- No automated entries, exits, or broker execution.
- No recommendation of what to trade next, what strategy to use, or what
  to study next — every figure is a backward-looking count of what you
  already did, never a forward-looking suggestion.
- No new database table — every figure is read live from the same tables
  each engine's own analytics module already reads: `investing_portfolios`,
  `investing_holdings`, `investing_research_notes`, `value_watchlist`,
  `investing_decision_snapshots`, `investing_risk_snapshots`,
  `investing_optimisation_reviews`, `investing_saved_screens`,
  `trading_positions`, `trading_trade_plans`, `trading_journal_entries`,
  `trading_strategies`, `trading_strategy_checklists`,
  `institutional_reports`, `platform_notifications`, and
  `learning_progress`.

Dashboard was expanded with a real Cross-Engine Snapshot, and
`docs/Executive-Reporting.md` for the Reporting Centre extension.
