# v1.4.0 — Learning Centre Sprint L2E: Trading Academy Foundations

**Branch:** `v1.4.0-learning-centre-l2e` (off `main`, post-Sprint-L2D merge
commit `1c29db8`)
**Scope, as explicitly approved:** begin the Trading Academy — a distinct
academy from the Platform Academy (Sprints L1/L2A–L2D), teaching
professional trading concepts through the platform's own real, existing
Market Structure/Liquidity/Risk Management engines rather than generic
theory. Four modules: Market Structure Fundamentals, Liquidity & Order
Flow, Volume Profile & VWAP, Risk Management. Do NOT merge into `main`.
Open a Pull Request and wait for approval.

---

## 1. Objective

Unlike the Platform Academy, which teaches operation of the software, the
Trading Academy teaches professional trading concepts demonstrated through
the platform's existing features. This sprint required the heaviest
honesty-discipline investigation of any Learning Centre sprint to date:
the brief's own requested vocabulary (Break of Structure, Change of
Character, buy-side/sell-side liquidity, liquidity sweeps, stop hunts,
Point of Control, Value Area High/Low, VWAP, Session VWAP) is real,
standard, professional trading terminology — but a large fraction of it
has **zero implementation anywhere in this codebase**, and one part of it
is explicitly, deliberately excluded by the codebase's own test suite.

## 2. Real Platform Analysis (performed before authoring any content)

Two parallel, read-only investigation passes were run against the real
source tree — one covering Market Structure/Liquidity/Order Flow, one
covering Volume Profile/VWAP/Risk Management — before any content was
written.

- **Market Structure Fundamentals.** `lib/tradingMarketStructure.ts`
  detects swing highs/lows via a fixed, non-configurable 5-candle
  symmetric window (`SWING_WINDOW = 2`), classifies trend
  (uptrend/downtrend/range) via strict monotonicity across the last 3
  swings (`RECENT_SWINGS = 3`), and clusters zones within 0.5% tolerance
  (`ZONE_TOLERANCE_PCT`). **The single most important finding of this
  sprint:** `lib/tradingStructureTimeline.ts` — the Structure Shift
  Timeline behind the Market Structure Workbench — reports exactly 9
  flat, generic event types (`higher_high | higher_low | lower_high |
  lower_low | trend_change | range_entry | range_exit | support_test |
  resistance_test`), and its own test suite (`tradingStructureTimeline.test.ts`)
  contains an explicit, permanent assertion — `expect(e.label).not.toMatch(/BOS|CHOCH|MSS/i)`
  — proven by the code's own comments to be deliberate: "events are named
  exactly as specified... never BOS/CHOCH/MSS or any other
  strategy-specific terminology, since none of those concepts exist
  canonically anywhere in this codebase." Break of Structure and Change of
  Character are real, standard concepts a Trading Academy cannot skip, but
  this platform guarantees, by its own test suite, that it will never
  compute or label either one.
- **Liquidity & Order Flow.** `lib/tradingLiquidity.ts` computes a real
  10-bucket Volume Profile (`VOLUME_PROFILE_BUCKETS = 10`, sorted
  strongest-first, top 8 returned), a Liquidity Band (High/Moderate/Low,
  `LIQUIDITY_HIGH_THRESHOLD = $25M` average daily dollar volume), and a
  Buy/Sell Pressure split (`PRESSURE_THRESHOLD_PCT = 55`, doji candles
  excluded from both sides). A repository-wide search (both `lib/` and
  `pages/`, all naming variants) confirmed **buy-side liquidity, sell-side
  liquidity, liquidity sweep, stop hunt, equal highs/lows, and liquidity
  pool are completely absent anywhere in this codebase, under any name,
  in any form** — not even a simulated approximation.
- **Volume Profile & VWAP.** The same 10-bucket Volume Profile from the
  Liquidity Engine is the platform's only real artifact in this space. A
  further repository-wide search confirmed **Point of Control, Value
  Area (VAH/VAL), VWAP, and Session VWAP are completely absent anywhere
  in this codebase, under any name, in any form** — zero matches for the
  terms or their underlying formulas, front or back end, simulated or
  real.
- **Risk Management.** `lib/tradingRisk.ts` computes dollar risk per
  position (`|entryPrice − stopPrice| × |quantity|`) against a
  `MAX_RISK_PER_POSITION_PCT = 2` cap, an aggregate portfolio risk budget
  against a `MAX_PORTFOLIO_RISK_PCT = 6` cap, with a hard-cap override
  (`overallScore = Math.min(overallScore, 60)` when either cap is
  breached) and a weighted blend (0.35 position sizing / 0.30 stop
  discipline / 0.35 portfolio budget). `lib/tradingDomainModel.ts`'s
  `computeRiskParameters()` computes the real R:R formula
  (`rewardDistance / stopDistance`). `lib/tradingScenarioComparison.ts`'s
  Scenario Comparison honestly identifies only the single scenario with
  the best R:R (`Best R:R`), never ranks or scores every scenario's R:R.
  Confirmed `stopPrice`/`targetPrice` are real, nullable `real` columns
  on the `trading_positions` table with **zero platform guidance on
  where to actually place a stop** — the platform scores discipline
  (whether a stop/target exists at all) and dollar risk (once one is
  entered), never prescribes a stop-placement technique.

## 3. Design decisions made during implementation

- **Upgrade-in-place vs. new topic, per the established L2A–L2D
  heuristic.** `trading-liquidity` and `trading-risk-management` already
  existed as short, plain topics directly matching 2 of the 4 requested
  modules — both were **upgraded in place** (same key, richer content,
  retitled). `trading-market-structure` (already rich, but focused on
  reading the Workbench's page mechanics rather than teaching HH/HL/LH/LL
  theory) and "Volume Profile & VWAP" (no prior coverage at all) both
  warranted genuinely **new** topics.
- **Honest disclosure over invented functionality, the heaviest
  discipline test of any Trading Academy module so far.** Every module
  states plainly, upfront, which parts of its requested vocabulary are
  real platform features and which are professional theory the platform
  does not implement:
  - `market-structure-fundamentals` teaches HH/HL/LH/LL and trend
    identification as genuine platform mechanics, but explicitly and
    prominently discloses that BoS/CHoCH are real concepts this platform
    deliberately never computes or labels — backed by the codebase's own
    permanent test assertion, cited directly in the lesson.
  - `trading-liquidity` (upgraded) teaches buy-side/sell-side liquidity,
    sweeps, and stop hunts as real professional vocabulary, with an
    explicit, upfront disclosure that none of them exist in this codebase
    under any name — the lesson instead documents the real Liquidity
    Engine (volume profile, liquidity band, buy/sell pressure) and
    explicitly warns against conflating a zone's "strength" (touch count)
    with a genuine liquidity concept.
  - `volume-profile-vwap` is the heaviest disclosure lesson of the
    sprint: it opens by stating plainly that POC/VAH/VAL/VWAP/Session
    VWAP are real concepts this platform computes **none** of, then
    offers the one legitimate, honestly-framed bridge available — the
    Volume Profile's #1 bucket is mathematically equivalent to a Point of
    Control, but is never labeled or special-cased as one by any platform
    code, so any "this is like a POC" reading is explicitly framed as the
    student's own interpretation layered on an honestly unlabeled list,
    never a platform claim.
  - `trading-risk-management` (upgraded) documents the real dollar-risk
    formula, the 2%/6% caps, and the hard-cap override, while explicitly
    disclosing that stop-loss placement technique has zero platform
    guidance — the platform only scores whether a stop exists and how
    much dollar risk it represents, never where to place one.
- **Placement, per the intra-path `nextStepKeys` constraint established
  in Sprint L2A.** All 4 modules live in the `trading-engine` path (the
  Trading Academy's own path, distinct from `platform-basics` and
  `institutional-investing`). `market-structure-fundamentals` was
  inserted immediately after `trading-market-structure` and before
  `trading-liquidity`, with the chain rewired:
  `trading-market-structure` → `market-structure-fundamentals` →
  `trading-liquidity` → `volume-profile-vwap` (end of that sub-chain).
  `trading-liquidity` and `trading-risk-management` kept their existing
  positions (upgraded in place, keys unchanged);
  `trading-risk-management`'s own existing `nextStepKeys` target
  (`trading-trade-planning`) is unchanged, now made explicit. No separate
  trading-engine-path chain-order test exists (only a platform-basics one
  does, confirmed unaffected — see §7), so this rewiring required no
  further test changes beyond the ones already made.
- **Glossary — a deliberate departure from L2A–L2D's zero-new-terms
  precedent.** This sprint introduces genuinely new vocabulary
  (BoS/CHoCH/swing points/POC/Value Area/VWAP) with no prior glossary
  coverage, so 6 new `"trading"`-category terms were added:
  `swing-high-low`, `break-of-structure`, `change-of-character`,
  `point-of-control`, `value-area`, `vwap` — each definition embeds the
  same honesty disclosure as its source lesson directly in its own text,
  so the disclosure survives even if a reader only ever sees the
  glossary card and never opens the full lesson. 10 existing keys were
  confirmed already present and reused as-is: `market-structure`,
  `support-resistance-zone`, `liquidity-band`, `volume-profile`,
  `buy-sell-pressure`, `trading-position-sizing`, `risk-reward-ratio`,
  `trading-capital-allocation`, `portfolio-risk-budget`, plus
  cross-references among the 6 new terms themselves.
