## Welcome to the Version 1.6 Beta

Version 1.6 is the first release focused entirely on how the platform
*feels* to use, rather than what it can do. Every underlying engine —
Options Income, Institutional Trading, Institutional Investing — is
unchanged; what's new is a real effort to make the platform easier to
learn, easier to navigate, and clearer about what to do next at every
step. This beta is your chance to tell us whether that effort actually
worked.

### A note on what this platform is and isn't

Every trade, order, and account balance you see in this beta is either
**SIMULATED** or **Paper Trading** — never real money, never a real
brokerage connection unless you've explicitly configured one yourself.
This has always been true and does not change in this release; it's
called out here because it's the single most important thing to
understand before you start.

## What's new

### The AI Trading Coach — a genuine guided workflow, not just a chatbot

The AI Trading Coach is a new kind of assistant on this platform: instead
of answering open-ended questions, it tracks your actual daily trading
workflow — an 11-step sequence from Morning Brief through Daily Review —
and always tells you the single next thing worth doing, grounded in your
own real account data. It never pressures you to trade; "No Trade Today"
is offered as an equally valid, professional outcome at every step.

In this beta, the coach also got a redesign: its full step-by-step
checklist now stays out of your way by default (one click reveals it),
so the page you actually came to see — Scanner, your Trading Journal,
wherever you land — is visible the moment you arrive, not buried below a
wall of coach content.

### Guided Workflow, everywhere you already work

Every major page on the platform — Scanner, Decision Workflow, Execution
& Lifecycle Manager, Trading Research, and more — now shows the same
13-stage journey breadcrumb (Discover → Research → Trade Plan → Decision
→ Execute → Portfolio → Journal → Learning), with your current stage
highlighted and the next one always named. Wherever you are, you can see
exactly where that page fits in your overall process.

### A platform-wide UX transformation

This release is the product of a dedicated usability pass, built on a
genuine first-time-user review of the entire platform — walking every
major page as though seeing it for the first time, scoring it, and
fixing what that review found. Concretely:

- Pages that used to bury their own content behind the AI Coach panel —
  most notably the Trading Journal and Daily Report — now show their own
  title and content immediately, without scrolling.
- Two pairs of easily-confused pages (Execution vs. Lifecycle Management;
  the two different "Trade Plan" systems) now each explain, in one
  sentence with a direct link, how they're different and which one you
  want.
- A real, previously-reported trust bug is fixed: the sidebar no longer
  ever shows "Sign in" for an account you're genuinely signed into.
- Blocked or not-applicable workflow steps now state their real reason
  inline (e.g. "Market is closed"), instead of an unexplained badge.

### Navigation improvements

Every sidebar link and section header now shows its full name on hover,
even where the visible label is shortened to fit — so nothing in the
sidebar is ever a mystery just because it's been truncated. The sidebar's
own structure is unchanged in this release; a deeper reorganization is
being considered for a future version based on what this beta surfaces.

### Contextual learning, right where you need it

The "Learn" trigger available throughout the platform now more often
points to the specific lesson relevant to what you're doing in the
moment — the workflow step you're on, the page you're viewing — rather
than a generic starting point, so the right explanation is usually one
click away instead of a search.

### Guided Tours for your first day

Four short, interactive tours — First Trade, First Research, First
Journal Entry, and First Portfolio Review — are available from the
Command Centre to walk you through the platform's own golden path the
first time you use it.

## What to expect from this beta

This is an honest beta, not a finished product demo. `docs/v1.6.0-Known-Limitations.md`
lists, in detail, what's intentionally unfinished, what's deferred to a
future release, and what you should simply ignore if you encounter it.
Please read it — it will save you time, and it tells you exactly what
kind of feedback is most valuable right now.

Thank you for being part of this beta. What you find in the next several
sessions will directly shape the next release.
