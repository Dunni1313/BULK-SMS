# Learning Centre Sprint L2K — Professional Institutional Workflows Academy

This academy teaches how a professional trader, portfolio manager, or investment analyst actually works this platform across a full daily/weekly/monthly cycle — real workflows over already-taught platform features, never a new capability of its own.

## 1. Workflow Capability Map

Before writing any content, all 15 requested workflow areas were investigated directly (source reading plus 2 parallel read-only Explore agents — one covering Morning Preparation/Research Workflow/Professional Trade Workflow, the other covering Portfolio Review/Options Workflow/Weekly & Monthly Review).

### Key findings

- **5 deliberately separate "start of day" dashboards** exist (Home at `/`, the real default landing page; Command Center; Institutional Dashboard; Executive Dashboard; the original Options Dashboard) — no single canonical screen. 3 independent market-regime reads exist (Portfolio AI's real LLM-narrated Market Briefing; Investing Macro Context, explicitly self-labeled SIMULATED; the Trading Engine's own price-action-derived Regime) — shown side by side in Cross-Engine Verdict/Macro-Regime sections, but never algorithmically merged. **News review is confirmed absent anywhere in the codebase.**
- **Research Workflow** is fully real end to end (Opportunity Discovery, Value Research's full report, 8-coach Institutional AI Coach, Ask/Narrate LLM buttons, Research Terminal Compare/Split modes, 4 real note-recording surfaces). The Cross-Engine Verdict (Engine 1 + Engine 2 shown together) is a real, disclosed **display juxtaposition only**, never a combined score.
- **Professional Trade Workflow**'s single most important nuance: the Strategy Academy/Library teach **9** named strategies, but the real, live scanner/execution engine only builds and prices **4** (`iron_condor`, `iron_fly`, `calendar_spread`, `earnings`). Alpaca's broker integration is hardcoded to the **paper-trading API host** — confirmed there is no live-market code path anywhere in this codebase.
- **Portfolio Review Workflow** spans two genuinely separate, never-blended accounts with two structurally different Health Score formulas (Investing's 3-component capped-blend Risk-tab score vs. Options' 8-factor executive Health Score). The Rebalancing Engine is real, but its own source header states it is **explicitly Investing-only** — Trading Engine and Options positions carry no stored target-weight field anywhere in this codebase.
- **Options Workflow**: Strategy Framework/Workbench are the Trading Engine's own generic methodology tool, **not** the options-strategy picker (that's the Scanner's own filter) — a real, confirmed point of confusion this sprint corrects. Options POP and the Trading Engine's own probability cone are two genuinely different calculations on two different engines. The Options Lifecycle Manager's `reviewCadence` field is a user-set **tag, never a scheduler**. Exit-planning mechanics are already fully taught in an existing topic (`portfolio-managing-positions`) — this sprint links to it rather than duplicating it.
- **Weekly & Monthly Review**'s most significant finding: a real, DB-backed **Workflow Center** (`portfolioWorkflows.ts`, Phase 44) genuinely ships named `weekly_review`/`monthly_review`/`quarterly_review` workflows with persisted step checklists and instances at `/portfolio-workspace` — **user-initiated only, never calendar-scheduled**. An *existing* Learning Centre topic (`portfolio-ai-review-workflow`) predates this feature and states no such thing exists — now stale, corrected in place this sprint (see §3). Performance review spans 4-5 genuinely distinct modules, one of which (`performanceAnalytics.ts`, `/performance`) is explicitly **synthetic RNG data**, confirmed by its own source header. No real (non-synthetic) win-rate-by-strategy view exists, and no persisted "improvement plan"/"action item" feature exists anywhere — both honestly taught as manual practices.

### Educational-only / partially-implemented items, honestly disclosed in the new content

- News review: confirmed absent, no in-app step exists.
- Session planning: only a narrow, read-only 4-session panel inside the Liquidity Workbench — no dedicated "plan my day" feature.
- 5 of the 9 taught options strategies are not live-tradeable by the real execution engine.
- Rebalancing never applies to Trading Engine or Options positions.
- Real (non-synthetic) per-strategy win rate: does not exist anywhere.
- Improvement/action-item planning: does not exist as a platform feature — manual practice only.
- Review-workflow cadence labels (Options Lifecycle Manager, Portfolio Workspace) are descriptive tags, never automated triggers.

No dashboards, AI capabilities, APIs, or workflows were invented. Every route, field, and behavior in the new/corrected content was confirmed against real source before being written.
