# Institutional Trading Analytics Engine

Phase 32. A deterministic analytics and reporting layer that aggregates
data already produced by every other Trading Engine module — the Trade
Workspace, Trade Planning Studio, Market Structure Workbench, Liquidity &
Session Workbench, Trading Journal, Strategy Framework, Strategy
Workbench, Trading AI Coach, and Learning Centre.

**This is an analytics and reporting phase, not a strategy or signal
phase.** Nothing here implements or evaluates ICT, SMC, ASAD, Trader
Bill's method, Tom Nash's method, the Dunni Framework, or any other named
methodology. No automated entries or exits, no trading signals, no
probability predictions, no broker execution. Every figure on this
dashboard is a direct read, count, tally, or simple aggregate of data you
already generated by using the platform — never a new calculation, score,
or forecast.

## Where to find it

`/trading-analytics`, linked from the sidebar navigation, the Command
Palette (inherits the nav entry automatically), the Trade Workspace, the
Institutional Strategy Workbench, the Trading AI Coach, the Learning
Centre overview, and the Investing Executive Dashboard's own
navigation-shortcut card.

## What it is

- **Analytics Overview** — trades reviewed, plans created, journal
  entries, workspace notes, strategies registered, checklist instances —
  the same figures every other engine already persists, simply counted.
- **Strategy Analytics** — checklist completion rate, evidence usage
  (which existing engines your strategies cite as required, and how many
  checklist items actually carry an attached evidence link), and a
  per-strategy checklist-instance table.
- **Journal Analytics** — mood tally, setup-type tally, lesson-recorded
  rate, and an R-multiple distribution — all read directly from your own
  Trading Journal entries.
- **Risk Analytics** — real, already-recorded Trade Plan parameters
  (account risk %, risk/reward ratio) and real open-position stop/target
  discipline. This is **not** a re-run of the live Trading Risk Engine —
  that is a real-time analysis, never a history log — it only aggregates
  parameters you already entered.
- **Learning Analytics** — lessons/glossary/strategies/coaches viewed,
  completed vs. remaining learning topics, and your weakest topics (paths
  below 50% completion, sorted lowest-first) — never a prediction of what
  to study next, just an honest sort of your own already-computed
  progress.
- **Coach Analytics** — how many times you've consulted each of the 9
  Trading AI Coaches, and which one most recently.
- **Session Analytics** — a heatmap of your own trade entries by real
  trading session (Asia, London, New York, Overlap), derived from your
  positions' own entry timestamps.
- **Structure & Liquidity Engine Usage** — since no structure/liquidity
  *analysis* is ever persisted (those engines compute live, on demand),
  these are honestly built from two real, persisted proxies instead: how
  many times you've consulted the Structure/Liquidity Trading AI Coach,
  and how many of your registered strategies cite Market
  Structure/Liquidity as required evidence.

## Reporting Centre integration

A new **Trading Analytics Summary Report** in the Reporting Centre
(`/reporting-centre?reportType=trading-analytics-summary`), reusing the
existing report framework — the same `InstitutionalReport`/section shape
every other report already uses, generated on demand, savable, and
comparable in the same "generate → review → save" workflow.

## What it deliberately does not do

- No trading signal, probability, or prediction of any kind, anywhere on
  this dashboard.
- No automated entries, exits, or broker execution.
- No recommendation of what to trade next, what strategy to use, or what
  to study next — every figure is a backward-looking count of what you
  already did, never a forward-looking suggestion.
- No new database table — every figure is read live from the same
  `trading_positions`, `trading_trade_plans`, `trading_journal_entries`,
  `trading_workspace_notes`, `trading_strategies`,
  `trading_strategy_checklists`, and `learning_progress` tables every
  other Trading Engine module already writes to.

See `docs/Analytics-Architecture.md` for how the aggregation engine is
built, and `docs/Analytics-Integration.md` for the full audit-to-
integration map.
