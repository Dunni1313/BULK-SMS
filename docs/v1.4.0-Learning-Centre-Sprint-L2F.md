# v1.4.0 — Learning Centre Sprint L2F: Options Academy Foundations

**Branch:** `v1.4.0-learning-centre-l2f` (off `main`, post-Sprint-L2E merge
commit `cc6bf64`)
**Scope, as explicitly approved:** begin the Options Academy — teaching
options concepts through the platform's own existing options tools,
scanners, analytics, calculators, portfolio views, and AI guidance. Five
modules: Options Fundamentals, Understanding the Greeks, Options Pricing/
Volatility/Probability, Options Chain and Contract Selection, Options Risk
Management. No strategy-specific lessons (iron condors, iron flies,
calendar spreads) beyond brief references. Do NOT merge into `main`. Open
a Pull Request and wait for approval.

---

## 1. Objective

Unlike the Platform and Trading Academies, the Options Academy needed a
mandatory first step before any content was written: a full inspection of
the platform's real options routes, pages, components, engines, schemas,
calculations, and tests, mapping every requested concept to real
functionality and identifying gaps honestly.

## 2. Platform implementation inspected

Three parallel, read-only investigation passes were run before any content
was written — covering (1) options fundamentals + the Option Chain page +
contract selection, (2) Greeks computation and display at both the
single-position and portfolio level, (3) pricing/volatility/probability and
options risk management. Key findings, each independently confirmed by
direct source inspection:

- **The single most important finding of this sprint:** a substantial
  pre-existing options-education structure already exists in
  `learningPaths.ts` — six top-level paths (`foundations`, `greeks`,
  `volatility`, `strategies`, `portfolio`, `performance`), ~40 short,
  plain topics predating the Sprint L1+ rich-content pattern, plus two
  dedicated standalone teaching pages (`/learn/delta` Delta Masterclass,
  `/learn/greeks` Greeks Tutor, backed by `coach.ts`'s AI-narrated
  `teachGreek()`). This governed every design decision below — none of it
  was duplicated.
- **Option Chain page (`/options/:symbol`)** — a real, working two-sided
  contract table (calls/strike/puts), showing Delta/Theta/IV/Bid/Mid/Ask
  per strike. Confirmed gaps: it shows exactly one hardcoded 45-DTE
  expiration (no expiration picker is wired up, despite an unused
  expirations endpoint existing); volume and open interest are not
  rendered as columns even though the underlying API returns them.
  Moneyness (ITM/ATM/OTM) is shown only as an unlabeled background-color
  tint, never a text badge.
- **Greeks** — `optionsMath.ts`'s `bs()` computes Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega
  only. **Rho is 100% absent from computation and every interactive
  feature** — not in the `Greeks` interface, not in `coach.ts`'s
  `GreekName` type, not selectable in the Greeks Tutor. A real, disclosed
  UI label/implementation mismatch was found: the Portfolio page's own
  "Beta-Weighted Delta" label is bound to a plain, un-weighted sum with no
  beta adjustment applied anywhere in the computation.
- **Pricing/Volatility/Probability** — Probability of Profit (POP) and
  Expected Value (EV) are real, computed model outputs with a disclosed
  volatility-risk-premium haircut; EV is used as a hard reject gate.
  Breakeven/Max Profit/Max Loss are exact structural math for iron
  condors/flies, honestly reported unavailable for calendars/earnings
  plays. **Historical Volatility (HV) is confirmed computed nowhere in the
  codebase** — despite the pre-existing `volatility-hv` topic and
  `historical-volatility` glossary entry both implying IV-vs-HV comparison
  was a real platform feature.
- **Options Risk Management** — `risk.ts`'s execution engine structurally
  blocks every naked (undefined-risk) position before it can ever be
  opened — a hard, enforced guarantee, not a warning. Assignment risk is a
  real, rule-based (DTE + moneyness) warning level, never a prediction.
  The Earnings & Event Risk overlay is explicitly self-labeled `"SIMULATED"`
  in its own underlying data — a calendar/timing overlay, never a live
  gap-detection or news feed. Liquidity gating is real and enforced
  server-side, but a rejected contract is silently filtered — never shown
  individually, only an aggregate Dashboard count reflects it.

## 3. Design decisions made during implementation

- **Upgrade-in-place vs. new topic.** Given the scale of pre-existing
  content (~40 plain topics across 6 paths substantially overlapping the
  5 requested modules), upgrading every atomic topic individually would
  have been far beyond a single sprint's scope. The design instead
  upgrades the single most representative existing topic per module where
  one exists, preserving its key, and cross-references (never duplicates)
  every sibling atomic topic:
  - `foundations-options` (Module 1) — upgraded in place into the full
    Options Fundamentals lesson. Sibling atomic topics
    (`foundations-calls`/`-puts`/`-strike-price`/`-premium`/`-expiration`/
    `-assignment`) stay untouched as quick-reference companions.
  - `greeks-portfolio-greeks` (Module 2) — upgraded in place into the
    practical single-position + portfolio + sensitivity + review-workflow
    lesson. `greeks-delta`/`-gamma`/`-theta`/`-vega` (which already link
    to the dedicated Masterclass/Tutor pages) and `greeks-rho` stay
    untouched — this lesson deliberately does not re-derive each Greek
    from scratch.
  - `volatility-pricing-probability` (Module 3) — a **new** topic, since
    no existing topic covered bid/ask/liquidity/volume/OI/POP/breakeven/
    max-profit/max-loss/EV/ROC together. `volatility-hv` also received a
    small, disclosed factual correction in place (still plain, key
    unchanged) — it previously implied HV comparison was a real platform
    feature.
  - `options-chain-contract-selection` (Module 4) — a **new** topic; no
    existing lesson walked through the actual chain-browsing screen.
  - `options-risk-management` (Module 5) — a **new** topic covering
    genuinely distinct, position-level/structural risk concepts (defined
    vs. undefined risk, assignment risk, liquidity risk as a single-trade
    concern), explicitly cross-referencing rather than duplicating the
    existing `portfolio` path's own whole-account risk lessons
    (concentration, diversification, correlation, buying power, stress
    testing, event risk).
- **Honest disclosure over invented functionality**, the heaviest
  discipline test of this sprint. Every module states plainly which parts
  of its requested vocabulary are real, computed platform features versus
  disclosed gaps: no expiration picker on the chain page; no volume/OI
  columns; no labeled moneyness badge; Rho absent from computation; the
  Beta-Weighted Delta label mismatch; HV computed nowhere; no per-contract
  liquidity-rejection UI; no automated exercise/assignment handling; the
  Earnings & Event Risk overlay's own SIMULATED self-label.
- **`nextStepKeys` same-path constraint, discovered and corrected during
  validation.** The existing `learningPaths.test.ts` enforces that a
  topic's `nextStepKeys` resolve to a real topic *within the same path*
  (`path.topics.some(...)`) — a stricter scope than this sprint's first
  draft assumed. `foundations-options`' initial `nextStepKeys` pointed
  cross-path into the `greeks` path; caught by a dedicated pre-test-suite
  verification script and corrected to `["foundations-calls"]`, chaining
  within the `foundations` path itself. `options-scanner-watchlists`
  (options-income-engine path) was rewired from an end-of-chain `[]` to
  `["options-chain-contract-selection"]`, continuing into
  `["options-risk-management"]`, which correctly ends the chain — all
  within the `options-income-engine` path.
- **Worked-example count constraint, discovered during content writing.**
  The existing `moduleGuideKeys` test enforces exactly 3 worked examples
  per module-guide topic, labeled exactly `Good Opportunity`/
  `Average Opportunity`/`Poor Opportunity`. Module 4's brief named 4
  distinct examples (directional call, directional put, deep ITM, rejected
  for liquidity); the deep-ITM example was folded into the same "Good
  Opportunity" worked example as the directional call (as a second
  scenario within its own `steps`) rather than a 4th separate entry,
  preserving the established, tested 3-example pattern.
- **Glossary — 8 new terms**, since this sprint introduces genuinely new
  vocabulary with no prior coverage: `option-intrinsic-value` (deliberately
  distinct from the pre-existing, unrelated `intrinsic-value` key under
  the Value Investing category — a real, checked-for collision risk, since
  that key means a company's own estimated fair worth, not an option's),
  `extrinsic-value`, `contract-multiplier`, `option-chain`,
  `bid-ask-spread`, `open-interest`, `breakeven`, `defined-risk`. 9
  existing glossary entries (`historical-volatility`, `assignment`,
  `buying-power`, `concentration`, `event-risk`, `max-profit`, `max-loss`,
  `probability-of-profit`, `expected-value`, `return-on-capital`) were
  updated in place — either a small honesty correction
  (`historical-volatility`) or an additive `relatedLessonKeys` entry
  pointing to this sprint's new/upgraded lessons, matching the established
  foundations/greeks/portfolio/performance category convention of tracking
  every lesson that covers a term.

## 6. Educational concepts intentionally left as references (not implemented)

Per the explicit honesty requirement, these real, standard professional
concepts are taught but explicitly, plainly disclosed as not implemented
by this platform, rather than invented or silently omitted:

- **Rho** — a real Greek, taught conceptually, but absent from every
  computation and interactive feature on this platform (confirmed by
  direct inspection of the pricing engine's own type definitions).
- **Historical Volatility (HV)** — a real, standard concept; this platform
  computes and displays IV and IV Rank only, never HV, anywhere.
- **Extrinsic value as a standalone field** — the concept is real and
  taught, but this platform never returns a standalone extrinsic-value
  number, only the total premium and, separately, intrinsic value.
- **An expiration picker on the Option Chain page** — the concept of
  browsing multiple expirations is real and standard; this platform's own
  chain page shows exactly one, hardcoded, expiration.
- **Volume and Open Interest as visible chain columns** — both concepts
  are taught and both are enforced server-side (as liquidity gates), but
  neither is rendered as a column on the Option Chain page itself.
- **Per-contract liquidity-rejection explanations** — the concept of
  seeing exactly why a specific contract was excluded is taught as a real
  professional practice; this platform only ever shows an aggregate
  rejection count, never a per-contract reason.
- **Automated exercise/assignment settlement** — taught as a real
  mechanic; this platform never automates it (only computes a risk-level
  warning), confirmed by direct inspection.
- **True Gap Risk (overnight/weekend statistical price-jump risk)** — the
  concept is taught; the closest real platform feature (the Earnings &
  Event Risk overlay) is a calendar/timing overlay, not a probabilistic
  gap-risk model, and is explicitly self-labeled SIMULATED in its own code.
