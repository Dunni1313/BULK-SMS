ADD-ON MODULE: BUFFETT-STYLE STOCK EDUCATION & RESEARCH

IMPORTANT:
This is an extension to the DK Stock + Options Suitability Engine.

Do NOT build a new app.
Do NOT rebuild existing features.
Reuse:

* Existing DK Option Engine
* Existing Stock Suitability Analyzer
* Existing AI Assistant
* Existing Portfolio AI
* Existing database
* Existing API architecture
* Existing dashboard framework

OBJECTIVE:
Add a Buffett-style stock research and education mode that helps the user understand whether a company is a high-quality long-term investment.

This should be educational and analytical, not hype-based.

The AI should teach the user how to think about a stock using long-term business-quality principles inspired by value investing.

Do not claim to be Warren Buffett.
Do not imitate a real person directly.
Use the wording:
“Buffett-style value investing framework”
or
“long-term value investor framework.”

CORE QUESTION:
For any stock, answer:

“Is this a wonderful business at a fair price, a fair business at a wonderful price, or a stock to avoid?”

MODULE 1 — VALUE INVESTOR RESEARCH MODE

Add AI Assistant mode:

Value Investor Research

The user can ask:

* Research Apple like a value investor
* Is Nvidia a wonderful business or too expensive?
* Would this stock fit a Buffett-style framework?
* Does this company have a moat?
* Is this stock undervalued?
* What is the margin of safety?
* Should I buy the shares, sell options, or wait?
* Teach me how to analyze this company
* Explain this stock like I am learning long-term investing

MODULE 2 — BUSINESS QUALITY ENGINE

Score business quality from 0–100.

Analyze:

* Revenue consistency
* Earnings consistency
* Free cash flow generation
* Profit margins
* Return on equity
* Return on invested capital
* Debt levels
* Cash position
* Pricing power
* Brand strength
* Competitive advantage
* Customer loyalty
* Recurring revenue
* Industry durability

Output:

Business Quality Score:
0–100

Rating:
Wonderful Business / Good Business / Average Business / Weak Business

Example:
AAPL
Business Quality: Wonderful Business
Reason: Strong brand, recurring ecosystem, high margins, strong cash generation.

MODULE 3 — ECONOMIC MOAT ENGINE

Analyze whether the company has a moat.

Moat categories:

* Brand moat
* Network effect
* Switching costs
* Cost advantage
* Scale advantage
* Intellectual property
* Distribution advantage
* Regulatory moat

Output:

Moat Rating:
Wide / Medium / Narrow / None

Moat Explanation:
Explain in plain English why the company does or does not have a durable advantage.

MODULE 4 — FINANCIAL STRENGTH ENGINE

Analyze:

* Debt to equity
* Interest coverage
* Free cash flow
* Cash reserves
* Operating margins
* Net margins
* ROE
* ROIC
* Share buybacks
* Dividend safety if relevant

Output:

Financial Strength:
Strong / Acceptable / Weak / Risky

MODULE 5 — VALUATION & MARGIN OF SAFETY ENGINE

Analyze:

* P/E
* Forward P/E
* PEG
* Price to sales
* Price to book
* Free cash flow yield
* Earnings yield
* Revenue growth vs valuation
* Fair value estimate if data allows
* Margin of safety

Output:

Valuation:
Cheap / Fair / Expensive / Very Expensive

Margin of Safety:
High / Medium / Low / None

Important:
Never fabricate fair value.
If fair value cannot be calculated from available data, show:
“Fair value unavailable — insufficient reliable data.”

MODULE 6 — VALUE INVESTOR DECISION ENGINE

Combine:

* Business Quality Score
* Moat Rating
* Financial Strength
* Valuation
* Margin of Safety
* Long-term trend
* Earnings risk
* News risk
* Portfolio concentration

Final decision:

* LONG-TERM BUY
* BUY ONLY ON PULLBACK
* WATCHLIST
* HOLD
* TRIM
* AVOID

Decision examples:

If quality is high but valuation is expensive:
Decision = BUY ONLY ON PULLBACK / WATCHLIST

If quality is high and valuation is fair:
Decision = LONG-TERM BUY

If business is weak but valuation is cheap:
Decision = AVOID or SPECULATIVE ONLY

If already owned and position is too large:
Decision = HOLD / TRIM

STOCK INVESTMENT / WATCH FOR OPTIONS
